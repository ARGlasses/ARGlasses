AR Glasses News: Industry Roadmaps, Products & Analysis

We track the companies, platforms and products shaping AI smart glasses, personal-display glasses and optical-see-through AR. The ten featured companies below are presented as an editorial watchlist, not a quality ranking.

1 · Meta24 September 2026

Meta keeps expanding the AI-glasses category

Connect 2026 expanded Meta’s AI-glasses line-up and announced new Muse experiences. Meta Ray-Ban Display also gained hands-free navigation and an Explore app, while its rollout expanded to the UK and Canada and then France, Italy and Germany from 13 October. Features and availability remain market-dependent.

5 · XREAL16 September 2026

XREAL is moving from personal displays toward Android XR

XREAL continues to develop personal-display glasses and its Android XR partnership. Its official 1 Pro+ page highlights X Prism optics, peak brightness up to 1,200 nits, spatial 3D at 60 fps and optional 6DoF anchoring with the separately sold XREAL Eye. The UK store lists £599 and currently invites customers to register for a stock notification, so listing price should not be mistaken for confirmed in-stock availability.
