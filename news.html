How we cover AR glasses

ARGlasses.com covers the wider smart-eyewear ecosystem while keeping product categories clear. Audio-only AI glasses, camera glasses, personal-display glasses, optical see-through AR and video-passthrough mixed-reality headsets are not interchangeable. We identify confirmed company announcements separately from editorial analysis, link to primary sources where possible and do not treat patents, rumours or analyst forecasts as confirmed product launches.

Our aim is to explain what is available now, what companies have publicly said they are developing, and what technical or commercial hurdles remain. Roadmap commentary is analysis, not a promise of future products or launch dates.
