QUICK ANSWERS

AR glasses: the questions people ask first.

What are AR glasses?

AR glasses are wearable devices that can present digital information while you view the physical world. Depending on the product, that can mean a virtual screen, AI assistance, optical see-through graphics or spatially aware content.

How are smart glasses different?

Smart glasses is a broad category. They can include cameras, microphones, speakers and AI without having a display. AR glasses specifically add visual digital content to your view.

Do AR glasses need a phone or computer?

Many do. The glasses may rely on a phone, laptop, handheld, console or companion computer for power, processing or content. Check the exact compatibility chain for the model you are considering.

What specifications matter most?

Look at the whole experience: display and optics, FOV, tracking, compatibility, weight and fit, battery or power, cameras and audio, prescription support and the required accessories.

What can AR glasses be used for?

Applications range from private screens and AI assistance to industrial fieldwork, collaboration, education, healthcare and retail. Capabilities vary substantially between products.
