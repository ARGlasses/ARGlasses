Cookie Policy for AR Glasses

Effective Date:2026

Website: https://www.arglasses.com

AR Glasses ("we," "our," or "us") uses limited browser storage to remember your privacy preference. The current site code does not use analytics or advertising tracking cookies. This policy explains the browser storage and external services used by the website.

You can review the details in the privacy dialog or manage browser storage through your browser settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in your browser. The current AR Glasses site code does not set its own analytics, advertising, login or session cookies. The privacy preference described below is stored in local storage instead.

2. Cookies and Browser Storage Used by This Site

At present, the site code does not set analytics, advertising, login, or session cookies. The cookie preference dialog stores your choice in your browser's local storage under the keys cookieConsent and cookiePreferences. Local storage is browser storage, not a cookie, and is used only to remember that the dialog has been answered.

The site does not currently load Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, or other analytics or advertising tracking tools. If these tools are introduced later, this policy and the preference controls will be updated and the tools will be configured to respect the applicable consent requirements.

3. Third-Party Services and External Resources

Some pages load resources or services from other providers. For example, the site loads web fonts and a CSS framework from third-party content delivery services, geographic map data from external data sources, and uses Formspree to process messages submitted through the contact form. When your browser connects to these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the requested resource. Their own privacy and cookie policies govern their services.

The current site code does not integrate Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel, Stripe, PayPal, or Mailchimp.

4. Privacy Preference Dialog

The dialog explains the current use of browser storage and third-party resources. Selecting Continue records that you have seen the notice; it does not grant permission for analytics or advertising tracking. The choice is stored in local storage on the device you are using, not in a cookie.

You can make the dialog appear again by clearing this website's local storage in your browser. If optional tracking is added in future, the dialog and its choices will be updated before those tools are enabled.

5. Managing Browser Storage and Cookies

You can clear this website's local storage and any cookies through your browser settings. Clearing local storage will cause the privacy dialog to appear again. Browser controls for third-party cookies may also affect resources and services supplied by other providers.

Instructions for managing cookies are available at:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated effective date.
