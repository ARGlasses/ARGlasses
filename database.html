Start with the category

“AR glasses” covers several very different products. The category is a starting point, not a quality rating.

Display glasses

Wearable screens for films, gaming and virtual monitors. Usually connected to another device.

AI smart glasses

Everyday eyewear built around cameras, microphones, speakers and AI; some also have a small display.

Spatial / true AR

Optical or see-through systems designed to place digital information into the physical environment using spatial sensing.

Enterprise AR

Rugged or workflow-focused systems for field service, training, inspection and industrial use.

How to read the important specifications

Resolution ≠ image quality

Resolution describes the number of pixels, but perceived clarity also depends on optics, lens design, panel quality, focus, text rendering and software.

FOV changes the experience

Field of view describes how large the visible image appears. A higher figure can mean a larger viewing area, but FOV measurements are not always reported using identical methods.

Brightness needs context

Manufacturer brightness figures may use different measurement conventions. “Nits” should therefore be compared carefully, especially between see-through and virtual-display products.

3DoF vs 6DoF

3DoF tracks orientation such as turning your head. 6DoF adds positional movement, allowing a system to understand changes in your location within its tracked space.

USB-C is not enough

A USB-C connector does not guarantee video output. Check for DisplayPort Alt Mode, the host device, adapters and any product-specific software requirements.

Weight is only part of comfort

Balance, nose pressure, temple pressure, cable position, lens fit, heat and prescription configuration can matter as much as the headline gram figure.

Database methodology

The Database is designed as a factual reference rather than a league table. It does not award an overall score or declare a single “best” product.

Source first

  • Manufacturer product pages are the primary reference.
  • Regional stores are used for published local pricing where available.
  • Unknown values remain unknown.

Comparable fields

  • Display, resolution, FOV and refresh rate.
  • Weight, tracking, power and cameras.
  • Audio, compatibility and prescription support.

Keep categories honest

  • Display glasses are not automatically spatial AR.
  • AI eyewear can have a display without being full AR.
  • Headsets are identified as such rather than presented as ordinary glasses.
