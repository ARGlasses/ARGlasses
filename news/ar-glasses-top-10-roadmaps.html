Top 10 roadmaps

AR Glasses Roadmaps: Companies, Products & Industry Direction

A compact roadmap page for the same ten companies featured on the main News page. The order is an editorial watchlist, not a quality ranking. Dates below are confirmed announcements or published market milestones where available; forward-looking items are labelled as expected, planned or developing.

ARGlasses Editorial Desk · Updated 1 October 2026

Meta

Meta is expanding from camera-and-audio AI glasses into display-equipped eyewear while continuing to broaden Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta distribution.

24 Sep 2026
Connect 2026

Meta announced new Ray-Ban Meta products, Muse integration and further display-glasses updates.

Autumn 2026
Display and AI expansion

Meta's next phase is centred on AI assistance, new eyewear formats and display products.

Source: Meta Newsroom

Snap

Snap is focused on true AR glasses, its Lens ecosystem and AI experiences that can understand context around the wearer.

16 Sep 2026
SPECS announced

Snap introduced SPECS Intelligence, new AR experiences and enterprise partnerships.

Later in 2026
Consumer rollout

Snap said SPECS would begin shipping later in the fall.

Source: Snap Newsroom

Google

Google's roadmap is ecosystem-led: Gemini, Android XR and hardware partnerships provide the software and AI layer for lightweight intelligent eyewear.

May–Jun 2026
Android XR expansion

Google detailed intelligent-eyewear plans and partnerships, including XREAL and Samsung.

Autumn 2026
Partner devices

Google's partner ecosystem is expected to bring the Android XR/Gemini experience to more glasses.

Source: Google

RayNeo

RayNeo's roadmap spans personal-display glasses and more capable optical products, with TCL's scale supporting wider distribution.

Q2 2026
Market momentum

IDC reported RayNeo at 3.6% of its Q2 smart-glasses market table; Omdia also highlighted RayNeo's tethered-glasses strength.

2026+
Display and AI

Watch the Air/GT families and continued work on lighter displays, AI and optical-see-through products.

Sources: IDC · Omdia

XREAL

XREAL is moving from a strong personal-display business toward broader spatial computing and Android XR.

2026
Next-generation display glasses

XREAL continues its One-family display strategy while developing new products for spatial computing.

2026+
Project Aura / Android XR

The Google partnership gives XREAL a route into more capable Android XR experiences.

Sources: XREAL · IDC

VITURE

VITURE's near-term roadmap remains centred on high-quality wearable displays for gaming, entertainment and multi-screen productivity.

1H 2026
Beast and Luma momentum

Omdia identified VITURE as one of the leading tethered XR-glasses vendors, with strong demand for the Beast series.

2026+
Software and retail expansion

Watch SpaceWalker and the company's expanding international retail footprint.

Sources: Omdia · VITURE

Rokid

Rokid is one of the key companies pushing waveguide-based AR glasses toward everyday AI and information displays.

2026
Waveguide and AI

Counterpoint continues to identify Rokid among the leading waveguide-based AR OEMs, with AI and lightweight form factors central to its strategy.

2026+
Broader consumer use

Watch international availability, navigation and contextual AI features as Rokid develops its glasses ecosystem.

Sources: Counterpoint Research · Rokid

Xiaomi

Xiaomi has the distribution, smartphone ecosystem and China market presence to make intelligent eyewear a natural extension of its device strategy.

Q2 2026
Top-five market presence

IDC reported Xiaomi at 2.3% of its Q2 2026 smart-glasses market table.

2026+
Ecosystem integration

Watch Xiaomi's glasses, AI services and connections with its wider phone and wearable ecosystem.

Source: IDC

Alibaba

Alibaba's differentiator is the combination of Qwen AI, cloud services and a huge China consumer ecosystem.

Q2 2026
Market entry gains scale

IDC ranked Alibaba third in its Q2 smart-glasses market table at 2.6%.

2026+
Qwen-powered eyewear

Watch how Alibaba connects AI assistance, translation, information display and commerce services to glasses.

Sources: IDC · Counterpoint Research

Even Realities

Even Realities is focused on lightweight waveguide eyewear designed to look and feel closer to conventional glasses.

2026
Waveguide category

Counterpoint identifies Even Realities as one of the important waveguide-based AR players alongside Meta, Rokid and Alibaba.

2026+
Information-first glasses

Watch navigation, notifications, translation and other contextual information features as the company expands its platform.

Source: Counterpoint Research
Editorial note: Roadmap dates are deliberately conservative. Confirmed announcements are separated from future-facing expectations, and market-share data is treated as a snapshot rather than a forecast. The order is an editorial watchlist, not a quality score or prediction.
