Company roadmap

VITURE AR Glasses Roadmap

VITURE is one of the major video-centric XR glasses brands, focused on entertainment, gaming and multi-screen productivity.

By ARGlasses Editorial Desk · Published 1 October 2026 · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Roadmap dates: confirmed milestones

These dates are based on public announcements and published industry research. They are milestones, not a promise that every future capability will arrive on a particular date.

2026
Beast and Luma families

VITURE continues to build its display-glasses portfolio around the Luma and Beast families. VITURE lists Beast at up to 1200p per eye, 120 Hz, a 58° field of view and up to 1,250 nits perceived brightness. These are manufacturer-listed peak specifications, not a guarantee of identical performance in every mode or environment.

2026+
Software + ecosystem

The company continues to develop SpaceWalker and its wider hardware/software ecosystem around wearable displays.

Sources: VITURE Beast specifications · Omdia, H1 2026 XR headwear

Market context

Omdia reported that RayNeo and VITURE together accounted for 93.1% of the tethered-display-glasses segment in H1 2026. VITURE held 8.6% of the broader XR-headwear market in that period. The segment share and overall-market share use different denominators and should not be treated as interchangeable rankings.

Software + ecosystem

The company is expanding SpaceWalker and its broader hardware/software ecosystem around wearable displays.

What to watch next

Watch for new hardware, optical improvements, AI capabilities, regional availability, prescription support and evidence of broader developer or ecosystem adoption. Forward-looking developments can change as companies update their plans.

Our outlook — analysis, not a launch prediction

The next stage of this roadmap will depend on product execution, software support, hardware constraints and how quickly useful experiences become practical for everyday wear. Treat unannounced dates and specifications as unconfirmed until the company publishes them.
