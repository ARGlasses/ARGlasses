Display glasses

Wearable screens for films, gaming and virtual monitors. Usually tethered to a phone, laptop or media device; they do not necessarily understand or map the room around you.

Examples: XREAL, VITURE, RayNeo Air, Rokid Max
✧

AI smart glasses

Everyday eyewear with cameras, microphones, speakers and an AI assistant. Some include a small private display; many have no visual display at all.

Examples: Ray-Ban Meta, Even Realities, Halliday
⌖

Spatial / true AR

Systems designed to anchor digital content in the physical environment using tracking, spatial mapping or optical see-through displays. Capabilities vary significantly.

Examples: Snap Specs, RayNeo X3 Pro, enterprise systems

HOW TO READ THE SPECS

Numbers need context.

Field of view (FOV)

The angle of the visible image. A wider FOV can make content feel larger, but the quoted diagonal figure is not the same as usable horizontal FOV.

Brightness

Check whether a figure is measured at the microdisplay or stated as perceived image brightness. Manufacturer methods can differ, so figures are not always directly comparable.

Refresh rate

A higher maximum refresh rate may require a supported host, resolution or mode. It does not guarantee every app runs at that rate.

Tracking

3DoF tracks rotation; 6DoF also tracks movement through space. Some glasses rely on a companion device or accessory for spatial features.

Our data and verification policy

We prioritise manufacturer product pages, technical specifications and support documentation. Independent reviews are used only to add context, not to silently replace a manufacturer's stated specification.

  • Not disclosed: the manufacturer does not publish a dependable figure in the sources checked.
  • Varies by configuration: the specification depends on size, lens, regional model or operating mode.
  • Announced / upcoming: the product has been announced but shipping specifications or broad retail availability may still change.
  • Price: UK RRP or current UK direct-store price when available; promotions and bundles can change. Always confirm on the retailer's page.
