The ARGlasses Certified Review Standard

“Certified” is an ARGlasses.com editorial designation, not a government, safety, medical or manufacturer certification. It means a review has met the publication's documented review requirements below. A product is never certified merely because its manufacturer supplied information.

Certified

Hands-on requirement

The reviewer must have access to the actual product and record first-hand observations. Manufacturer demonstrations or marketing material alone cannot qualify a review as Certified.

Certified

Evidence requirement

Published specifications are checked against primary manufacturer material. Where a claim depends on software, firmware, an accessory or a particular host device, that dependency is identified.

Certified

Repeatable test notes

Core tests are documented so the review can be understood and, where practical, repeated: display, fit, comfort, connection, tracking, audio, camera and everyday use.

Certified

Limitations disclosed

A Certified Review must document meaningful compromises rather than presenting a product as universally suitable. Missing or unverified information is labelled rather than guessed.

Certified

Date and software state

Reviews record the review date and relevant firmware/software state because connected glasses can change substantially after launch.

Certified

No pay-for-rating

Certification is an editorial process. It is not purchased by a manufacturer, and receiving a review unit does not guarantee favourable coverage.

Important: We will not invent hands-on findings. If a product has only been assessed from published information, it is labelled a Source Profile rather than a Certified Review.

What a Certified Review actually tests

1. Fit & comfort

  • Weight and balance
  • Nose and temple pressure
  • Lens position and adjustability
  • Heat and long-session comfort
  • Prescription-lens practicality

2. Display & optics

  • Clarity and text readability
  • Effective viewing area
  • Brightness in indoor/outdoor conditions
  • Colour, contrast and black levels
  • Edge clarity and optical artefacts

3. Tracking & spatial behaviour

  • 3DoF or 6DoF behaviour
  • Stability while moving
  • Anchor drift where applicable
  • Latency and responsiveness
  • Room/environment requirements

4. Compatibility

  • USB-C video behaviour
  • Phone, tablet and computer testing
  • Adapters and accessories
  • Operating-system requirements
  • Companion-app reliability

5. Audio, camera & controls

  • Speaker clarity and leakage
  • Microphone quality
  • Camera framing and capture
  • Touch, voice and gesture controls
  • Control reliability in normal use

6. Everyday ownership

  • Battery behaviour
  • Charging and cable management
  • Setup friction
  • Software updates
  • Case, accessories and portability

Review methodology and editorial safeguards

Primary sources first

Manufacturer documentation is used for technical specifications because it is the closest source to the product's intended configuration. Independent testing is used for experiential findings.

Facts vs experience

“We observed” and “the manufacturer states” are treated as different kinds of evidence. The review will not quietly convert a specification into a performance conclusion.

Software matters

Connected glasses can change through firmware and companion apps. A review records the relevant software state and may be updated when material changes alter the experience.

Accessories matter

Adapters, neckbands, battery accessories, prescription inserts and companion controllers can materially change the experience. Reviews identify when they are required.

No universal winner

Different glasses solve different problems. Reviews therefore focus on documented strengths, limitations and suitable use cases rather than forcing every product into one overall score.

Corrections welcome

If a published specification or observation is shown to be incorrect, the page should record the correction and update the underlying source information.

AR glasses review FAQs

Are these hands-on AR glasses reviews?

No. The products currently listed are labelled Source Profiles because they summarise published information. We do not claim first-hand experience or assign review scores where physical testing has not been completed.

What does Certified mean here?

It is an ARGlasses.com editorial designation for a review that meets the published hands-on, evidence, repeatability and disclosure requirements. It is not a manufacturer, government or safety certification.

How should I choose between different glasses?

Start with your main task, then check the display type, tracking, device compatibility, comfort, privacy, total accessory cost and current regional availability. A virtual-screen product and optical AR system may solve different problems.

Where can I compare specifications?

Use the AR glasses database for structured product details and source links, and the comparison tool to view selected models side by side.
