THE CHOICE

There are two futures inside the same pair of glasses.

More intelligence is not automatically more freedom. The difference is design: who owns memory, where data is processed, whether recording is visible, and whether the system can truly be switched off.

THE GOOD VERSION

Technology quietly amplifies agency.

It helps when invited, stays out of the way when not, and makes people more capable without making them more dependent.
