THE CHOICE

There are two futures hiding inside the same pair of glasses.

More intelligence is not automatically more freedom. The optimistic future depends on local processing, visible recording, user-owned memory, explainable actions and a genuine off switch.

THE GOOD VERSION

Technology quietly amplifies agency. It helps when invited, stays out of the way when not, and makes people more capable without making them more dependent.
